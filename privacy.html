Privacy Policy

Williams Marketing and Management, Inc (WMAMinc) · Last updated October 18, 2026

Who we are

Williams Marketing and Management, Inc ("WMAMinc", "we", "us") is a Texas company. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, our back office, and our business messaging (including WhatsApp), and how we use and protect it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number and email address.
  • Messages you send us, including on WhatsApp, and any photos, files or voice notes you choose to send.
  • Work information for our team members and contractors, such as assignments, schedules and records needed to run our programs.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you use our website, such as device and browser type.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages and requests, including with the help of our automated assistant.
  • To run our business: scheduling, inventory, onboarding, payroll records and reporting for our team.
  • To send you messages you asked for or that relate to your work with us.
  • To keep our systems secure and prevent misuse.

WhatsApp messaging

When you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) delivers your message to us. We use your messages only to reply to you and to handle what you asked for. We do not sell your messages or phone number, and we do not use them for advertising. Your use of WhatsApp is also covered by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (for example hosting, database, messaging and AI processing providers), under their duty to protect it, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete it.

Your choices

  • You can stop messaging us at any time, or ask us to stop messaging you.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections. Passwords and payment card numbers are never shared with our automated assistant.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: email wmaminc@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
