$600 – $1,000 / week · Performance Based

You are the first line of contact for new and existing customers. Learn, memorize, and demonstrate our proven customer service and sales principles for Fortune 500 clients — then help manage your own field marketing campaign. Student mentality and elite service skills are the only prerequisites.
